WHY SENTINEL EXISTS

Good decisions start
with a little more clarity.

Sentinel Business Shield is a startup-focused command center concept designed to make important work easier to see. Security signals, operational tasks, customer records, files, and basic runway planning all have a place in one interface.

It is built to help founders and small teams stay organized as they grow—not to replace dedicated security, finance, or legal professionals. The dashboard can be adapted to a team's workflow, and its AI assistant is optional.
